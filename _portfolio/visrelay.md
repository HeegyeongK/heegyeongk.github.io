**Status:** Ongoing research.

VisRelay studies how cooperative UAVs can use intermediate relay viewpoints to preserve sensing continuity when direct observation or direct handoff is disrupted by extended occlusion or difficult geometry.

The current formulation focuses on graph-based representation of candidate relay viewpoints, visibility and traversability constraints, and route selection for cooperative sensing.
