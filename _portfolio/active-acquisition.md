---
title: "Active target acquisition during multi-UAV handoff"
excerpt: "Receiver-side visibility recovery when the target is outside the field of view or physically occluded."
collection: portfolio
permalink: /research/active-acquisition/
---

A receiving UAV may have no local target-observation history when a handoff begins. Even while the sender continues tracking, the receiver can fail to acquire the target because of field-of-view limitations or environmental occlusion.

This work studies sender-guided first acquisition. The receiver distinguishes field-of-view failure from physical occlusion, proposes failure-specific recovery motion, and evaluates candidate motion under handoff-aware constraints before execution.

The method has been evaluated in closed-loop simulation and physical two-UAV field experiments. Public details are intentionally kept concise while the manuscript is under review.
