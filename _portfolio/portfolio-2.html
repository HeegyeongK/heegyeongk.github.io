--- title: "PATH — Geometry-assisted target handoff verification" excerpt: "Cross-view geometric verification that a receiver UAV has acquired the same physical target as the sender." collection: portfolio permalink: /research/path/ ---

During UAV handoff, the receiver may detect several visually similar objects from a different and continuously changing viewpoint. Detection alone does not verify target identity.

PATH uses inter-UAV geometry to project the sender's target information into the receiver's image as a spatial prior. The receiver returns a candidate observation, and the sender verifies agreement before tracking responsibility is transferred.

Real-world UAV experiments measured mean relative-position and target-position errors of 0.047 m and 0.030 m. The reported implementation operates at video rate with compact inter-UAV communication below 16 kB/s at 60 Hz.
